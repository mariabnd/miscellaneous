# Reminder of options

## Eqnarray or align

**Align** is considered a better option cf. [https://tex.stackexchange.com/questions/196/eqnarray-vs-align](StackExchange: eqnarray vs align)

## Natbib or biblatex

**Biblatex** is being maintained while natbib is not, other considerations here: [https://tex.stackexchange.com/questions/25701/bibtex-vs-biber-and-biblatex-vs-natbib](StackExchange: bibtex vs. biber and biblatex vs. natbib)
